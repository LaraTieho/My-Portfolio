ABOUT ME

Bridging the Gap Between Field Reality and Data-Driven Policy

I'm a Geomatics professional with eight-plus years of progressive experience integrating classical land surveying with modern GIS, remote sensing, and spatial analytics. My applied focus spans public health systems, humanitarian response, disaster risk reduction, and infrastructure planning — working with privite sector, ministries, donor funded projects from organisations, and humanitarian partners to turn complex spatial data into decisions that matter.

Currently finalised phase one of the Geospatial project, consulting with GDI Advisors on national health catchment delineation and digital microplanning for Lesotho's Ministry of Health, and previously with GIZ on national NSDI and geospatial standards assessment. I hold a BSc in Geomatics from the University of Cape Town, specialising in GIS and Land Survey, and a Postgraduate Diploma in MEAL from the Humanitarian Global Institute.
